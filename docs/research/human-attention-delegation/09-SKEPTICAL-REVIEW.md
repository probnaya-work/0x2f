# 09 — Skeptical review

An independent reviewer examined initial notes, source, fixture scripts/results and the emerging recommendations before numbered-report synthesis. [Its complete review](notes/skeptical-review-independent.md) is retained, including its own corrected inference. A [final accuracy pass](notes/skeptical-final-pass.md) then checked the numbered reports; its concrete corrections are incorporated. This is independent model reasoning, not a human peer review or substitute for user research.

## What the reviewer challenged

**A justified repair is not a validated business.** Missing written results and false current-run steering claims are concrete fidelity issues. Fixing them could improve a personal instrument even if no market or separate-product need exists. Do not present bug evidence as proof of the founder's strategic thesis.

**“Less attention” can mean worse judgment.** Faster acceptance, lower self-reported effort and fewer approvals may conceal missed caveats or deference. Review/merge were excluded from a particularly relevant parallel-agent study. The evaluation must include accurate acceptance and integration, not only dispatch/throughput.

**The permission boundary deserves higher priority.** Remote minimization drops full input and clips scope while keeping ALLOW possible. No real mistaken approval was observed, but representative permission-sufficiency review belongs alongside TUI and note-timing fidelity, ahead of speculative routing improvements. Exporting all raw input is not an acceptable automatic answer.

**Authentication readiness cannot be inferred from installed binaries or token-file presence.** We narrowed the recommendation from “auth-available defaults” to honest detection, selected-provider facts and actionable failure diagnostics. Default selection changes require first-use evidence.

**Another app may be unnecessary.** A native template, extension or local record exporter could capture much of the value without a new inbox or four surfaces. Native-plus-template is now a required baseline, and voluntary second-task choice is a required signal.

## Evidence gaps that prevent stronger conclusions

No actual interviews, unfamiliar-user sessions, human active-time/comprehension results, retained-use/adoption measures, willingness-to-pay data, physical-phone evaluation, broad desktop browser trial or direct competitor UX trial. Public issues are selected reports. Fixtures match literal strings and cannot establish a real model's constraint use. Fresh-process reopening is not a delayed-memory experiment. Synthetic history size is not observed context failure. Scripted command/intervention counts omit setup/checking and cannot measure attention debt.

The founder's industrial taste is a legitimate constraint, not proof of market demand. A primitive aesthetic can coexist with omitted essential information; visual restraint alone neither causes nor prevents usability. Commercialization, subscriptions, teams and integrations are not implied by a coherent personal tool.

## What would change each major recommendation

| Recommendation | Reversal/alteration evidence |
|---|---|
| Restore existing brief/result access | A verified reachable TUI path already does so, or the surface is intentionally retired |
| Correct note timing and audit permission scope | Demonstrated acknowledged current-run delivery; representative real permission shapes already carry all consequential detail |
| Trial concise return account | Current/native/template conditions yield equal or better accurate understanding at less total cost; summary loses important caveats |
| Honest diagnostics/default simplification | Unfamiliar users predict and recover correctly already; extra labels/preflight add burden without benefit |
| Keep alert expansion bounded | Physical-device/closed-client observation reveals repeated missed consequential stops with a narrow effective remedy |
| Defer orchestration/context machinery | Real repeated histories/concurrent tasks cause failures and a small isolated policy reduces total work safely |
| Continue as separate product | Requires repeated voluntary choice plus lower total effort without worse judgment; equality favors a smaller native extension/personal tool |

The strongest disagreement is strategic: even a perfectly faithful return surface may not overcome upstream distribution and the cost of another layer. Keep that possibility open. The credible verdict is **a coherent local task instrument with demonstrated continuity and fixable fidelity gaps; its attention-saving product necessity remains unproven**.
